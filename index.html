3. Part III — Months 7–24 Long-Term AI Infrastructure Specialization Blueprint

Months 7–9 · Mini Transformer Inference Runtime (Project 5)

Extend Week 21–24 attention & KV-cache primitives into a standalone C++/CUDA inference runtime executing a small pretrained decoder-only transformer (e.g., GPT-2 / Llama-architecture small weights). Implement embeddings, RMSNorm/LayerNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, prefill vs. decode phases, and benchmark latency/memory bandwidth.

Months 9–12 · Inference Serving Engine & Triton (Project 6)

Turn the runtime into a multi-tenant serving engine: request queue, dynamic & continuous (iteration-level) batching, PagedAttention block-table KV-cache allocator, preemption/reclamation. Measure TTFT, ITL (inter-token latency), throughput (tok/s), and p99 tail latency. Write custom Triton kernels and study vLLM & llama.cpp internals.

Months 12–18 · Distributed Training & Collective Comm (Project 7)

Move beyond single-GPU constraints on multi-GPU cloud/lab nodes: multi-process workers, ring All-Reduce, NCCL collectives, DDP gradient bucketing & comm/compute overlap, FSDP / ZeRO stage 1–3 parameter sharding, and multi-GPU scaling efficiency experiments. Read ZeRO & Megatron-LM papers.

Months 18–24 · Distributed Inference & Research Depth (Project 8)

Scale inference across multiple GPUs: Tensor Parallelism (column/row-parallel linear layers with All-Reduce), Pipeline Parallelism (micro-batch scheduling, bubble minimization), disaggregated prefill/decode exploration, and upstream open-source contributions (vLLM, CUTLASS, Triton, PyTorch).
